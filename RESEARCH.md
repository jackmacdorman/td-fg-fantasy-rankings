# Research notes — TD/FG-only league, 2026 season

## Scoring
Passing TD 3 · Rushing TD 6 · Receiving TD 6 · FG 3 (any distance) · PAT 1 · D/ST TD 6 · Safety 2.
Nothing else scores. Roster: 1QB, 2RB, 2WR, 1TE, 1D/ST, 1K. 12 teams.

## Quarterbacks

**Josh Allen is a tier of one.** 153 projected points vs. 123 for QB2 — a 30-point gap, larger
than the gap from QB2 all the way down to QB12. His 12 rushing touchdowns alone (72 points)
outscore 20 of the 40 quarterbacks on the board *in total*.

**The tush push survived the 2026 rules vote** (competition committee is not proposing a ban; the
2025 vote failed 22-10, short of the 24 needed). This preserves the Hurts/Allen goal-line sneak,
which is the single highest-leverage fact for this format.

**Rushing QBs are radically underpriced here.** A rushing TD is worth 2× a passing TD, so:
- Jaxson Dart (NYG) grades QB8 on ~6 rushing TDs. Biggest arbitrage on the board — but new HC
  John Harbaugh spent a decade teaching Lamar Jackson to slide, so this is also the highest-variance pick.
- Daniel Jones (IND) grades QB17 on 5-7 projected rushing TDs. Nobody drafts him for that.
- Justin Fields (KC) is the best handcuff in the format — a rushing QB behind a quarterback
  coming off ACL+LCL surgery who has not played a preseason snap.
- Malik Willis (MIA) is a pure lottery ticket: Yahoo projects 7 rushing TDs, Razzball 2.8.

**Fade pocket passers relative to normal fantasy.** Stafford (34 pass TD, 0.5 rush) and Goff
(30/0.5) are elite in standard leagues and only mid-pack here.

### Vegas coverage (thin)
Only four real season-long passing TD lines found: Allen O24.5, Prescott O27.5, Herbert O22.5,
Stroud U22.5. **Zero QB rushing TD season props exist at any book** — confirmed, FanDuel carries
no rushing prop even for Hurts. Since rushing TDs carry most of the weight for mobile QBs, that
is the largest single uncertainty on the board. RotoWire's passing-TD futures table was
JS-loaded and unscrapeable; pulling it would require logging into a book directly.

### Unsettled jobs
ATL (Tua by default, Penix on torn ACL), CLE (Watson named starter but Clay projects Sanders more
volume — sources conflict badly), LV (Cousins listed, Mendoza expected to take over), ARI
(Brissett opener, rookie Beck live). MIN resolved: Murray over McCarthy.

## Running backs

The whole position reduces to one question: **who gets the ball inside the five?** Yardage is
worth nothing, so a goal-line specialist on a bad offense beats a 1,400-yard back who scores five times.

### Vegas coverage (the best of any position)
Real season-long rushing TD lines exist for nine backs: Henry 12.5, Taylor 11.5, Cook 10.5,
Kyren Williams 9.5, Javonte Williams 9.5, Montgomery 7.5, Chase Brown 5.5, Achane 5.5, Mason 4.5.
Receiving TD props for three: Gibbs O4.5, McCaffrey O4.5, Bijan O3.5.

Where the market disagrees with the projection consensus: books are **lower** on Chase Brown
(5.5 vs 7.4) and Jordan Mason (4.5 vs 5.7), and **higher** on Derrick Henry. Trust the money.

Kalshi season rushing-TD ladders were pulled but deliberately excluded — zero volume, 20-40 cent
spreads, and non-monotonic pricing (Henry's 12+ traded above his 10+). Useful for ordering, not
for point estimates.

### Sleepers the projections systematically miss
- **Sean Tucker (TB)** — the single biggest value in the format. Bucky Irving had **zero carries
  inside the 5-yard line in 2025**; Tucker took nearly all of them and scored 8 TDs, 6 in his final
  8 games. Tampa re-signed him specifically for that role. His ~2.8 projected TDs is a model
  artifact of low carry volume. Draft him far above his row.
- **Chris Rodriguez Jr. (JAX)** — hand-picked by Liam Coen as the goal-line back over Tuten.

### Traps
- **Bucky Irving (TB)** — see above. Costs a real pick, doesn't get the goal line.
- **Saquon Barkley (PHI)** — Hurts vultures 8.5 rushing TDs and Barkley's price does not reflect it.
  (James Cook has the same Josh Allen problem, but his 10.5 line already prices it in.)
- **Bijan Robinson (ATL)** — took only 48% of Atlanta's inside-5 carries in 2025, and they signed
  Brian Robinson Jr. Committee, not bell-cow.
- **Kyren Williams / Blake Corum (LAR)** — literally alternate drives; reporting says Davante Adams
  is the actual goal-line target.

### Injuries and unsettled backfields

aggressive, FFToday's 3 is closer. Breece Hall strained a groin Aug 17, expected back for Week 1.
Genuinely unsettled rooms: SEA, WAS, PIT (true 50-50 Warren/Dowdle), CAR, ARI, KC, NO, TEN.

### Gaps

## Kickers and D/ST

**Kickers matter far more than defenses.** Kicker range is 101–167 points (66-point spread).
D/ST range is 10.7–21.8 (11-point spread). The gap between K1 and K32 is roughly six times
the gap between D1 and D32. Draft kickers aggressively; treat D/ST as a late dart throw.

**FG-lean is the format-specific edge.** Because a FG is worth 3 and a PAT only 1, an offense
that stalls in the red zone is better for you than one that scores touchdowns. Measured as
`3×FG / (3×FG + PAT)`:
- Highest lean: Fairbairn .50, Butker .49, Dicker .48, Slye .46
- Lowest lean: Mevis .35, Bass .36, Bates .37

Houston led the NFL with 52 FG attempts in 2025. Buffalo attempted a league-low 21 — Tyler Bass
ranks fine in standard formats and is a trap here.

**Daniel Carlson (NO) is the stalest number on the board.** Signed Aug 24, 2026; every published
projection was built on Charlie Smyth. New Orleans took 42 FG attempts in 2025 (T-3rd) at a
league-worst 71.4% conversion. An accurate kicker on that volume is unpriced.

**2025 team FG attempts** (proxy for red-zone stalling; the direct RZ TD% tables were all paywalled):
- Elite volume 38+: HOU 52, SEA 48, DAL 42, NO 42, LAC 41, CHI 39, ATL 39, KC 38, TB 38
- Avoid ≤26: NYG 26, BUF 21

### D/ST specifics
Defensive TDs are extremely noisy year to year (DAL 6→1→1, DEN 1→5→1, MIA 4→0→0), so
projections regress 50% to the league mean of ~1.6 def TDs/team/season.

Return TDs shifted sharply in 2025: punt return TDs jumped 6→15 while kick return TDs fell 8→6.
**Punt return talent, not kick return talent, is where the 6-point spikes come from.**

2026 kickoff rules: dynamic kickoff retained, touchback still the 35, onside legal any time,
receiving-team alignment slightly loosened. Net effect neutral — do not model a KR TD spike.

Philadelphia is the D/ST trap: top-5 in every conventional ranking (sacks, points allowed, both
worth zero here), 25th in this format — no return threat and only 5 defensive TDs in three years.

## Data caveats
- No Vegas season-long kicker props exist; books only post weekly in-season FG totals.
- FOX Sports return-stat pages label players by 2026 team, not the team they scored for —
  cross-referenced against 2026 PR/KR depth charts instead.
- NFL.com lists the 2025 Jets at 0 interceptions, which is not plausible; treated as low-but-nonzero.
- Safety projections are the softest numbers here (~14 safeties league-wide per season);
  they move team totals by under a point. Treat as noise.
